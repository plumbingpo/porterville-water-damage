Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Across Porterville, Porterville Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle sewage cleanup from start to finish:

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Porterville

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
